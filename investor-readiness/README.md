# B9TECH CONSULTS — Investor Readiness Workroom

**Status:** Internal working documents  
**Prepared:** 3 November 2026  
**Distribution:** Not approved for external circulation

This folder is the working source of truth for B9TECH's corporate capability and investor materials. It intentionally separates confirmed facts, management assertions requiring evidence, and strategic hypotheses.

## Workstreams

1. Corporate identity and legal standing
2. Enterprise capability and delivery model
3. B3 product and intellectual-property position
4. Market, customer discovery and competitive position
5. Revenue model and unit economics
6. Financial history, forecast and funding requirement
7. Governance, risk, privacy and security
8. Investor narrative and data-room readiness

## Documents

- [Company fact register](./company-fact-register.md): information required before external claims can be made.
- [Capability statement draft](./capability-statement-draft.md): buyer-facing corporate profile.
- [Investor brief draft](./investor-brief-draft.md): investment thesis and pitch structure.
- [Data-room index](./data-room-index.md): evidence checklist for due diligence.

## Publication rule

Do not convert draft materials into public claims, forecasts or investment solicitation until the underlying evidence has been reviewed and approved. Never invent customers, revenue, users, transaction volume, partnerships, certifications, market share, valuation or product functionality.
