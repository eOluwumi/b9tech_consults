### Company overview

B9TECH CONSULTS is a Nigeria-based technology consultancy and product-development business. Its current public positioning focuses on responsible AI, data science, software and digital product engineering, cybersecurity-aware technology delivery, workflow automation and technology advisory.

B9TECH's public product initiative is B3: BringBackBarter, a digital barter and exchange platform.

### Engagement approach

1. **Discover:** understand the business context, users, constraints and desired outcomes.
2. **Define:** agree scope, assumptions, risks, success measures and milestones.
3. **Deliver:** work in manageable stages with documented decisions and review.
4. **Transfer:** provide agreed documentation, knowledge transfer and support expectations.
