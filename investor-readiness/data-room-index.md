## Data handling rules

- Redact personal data, bank credentials, API keys, passwords and unrelated customer information.
- Do not upload raw production datasets or secrets into an investor room.
- Use read-only access, named recipients, expiry dates and access logs where available.
- Keep a record of each disclosure and any confidentiality restrictions.
- Separate public marketing materials from confidential diligence documents.

## Legal review flag

The current website privacy policy and B3 terms contain statements about data processing, AI/ML, geolocation, third-party services and legal compliance that must be checked against actual product behaviour and current legal requirements by qualified counsel. This index is not a legal opinion.
